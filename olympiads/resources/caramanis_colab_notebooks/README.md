# Caramanis Colab Notebooks

Downloaded from the two Greek course series on Constantine Caramanis' course
sites:

- `MachineLearningClass`
- `GenerativeAIClass`

Excluded on purpose:

- `MachineLearningMath`
- lecture pages that do not expose a public Colab link on the site
- duplicate Colab links that point to the same notebook

## Folder Layout

- `supervised_learning_and_ml/`
- `generative_ai_and_nlp/`
- `manifest.json`

## Counts

- `supervised_learning_and_ml`: 12 notebooks
- `generative_ai_and_nlp`: 9 notebooks
- `supervised_learning_and_ml/slides`: 9 slide files
- `generative_ai_and_nlp/slides`: 13 slide files

## Notes

- Filenames were normalized for easier local use.
- `manifest.json` keeps the original public Colab URL, Google Drive file id,
  saved relative path, and byte size for each notebook.
- The slide downloads include the individual public PDF files listed on the two
  course pages.
- The `GenerativeAIClass` page exposes a `LectureSlides.zip` link, but that URL
  currently returns `404`; this is recorded in `manifest.json` under
  `missing_published_slide_assets`.
- The `MachineLearningClass` ZIP bundle was intentionally not used in the final
  slide archive after your correction. The saved slide collection is PDF-only.
