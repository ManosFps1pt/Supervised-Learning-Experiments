# CEOAI-First Competition Samples

This archive is a CEOAI-first collection of public IOAI, regional, and national AI olympiad tasks.

Use it as a practice selector, not as a reading library. The target is to turn past-task style material into visible CEOAI evidence: notebook outputs, metrics, predictions, submission files, or short comparison notes.

## Files

- `source_index.csv` is the source-of-truth index. Every row has a source URL, CEOAI tag, local path when available, and download status.
- `ceoai_task_map.md` groups discovered tasks by CEOAI syllabus section.
- `practice_queue.md` ranks the most useful next tasks for a CEOAI sprint.
- `task_cards/` contains compact cards for high-value tasks.
- `raw/` stores downloaded public repositories and direct official zip files.

## Raw Archive Policy

Downloaded:

- Official IOAI 2025 sparse repository: task/solution notebooks, README, license, requirements.
- Official IOAI 2024 sparse repository: on-site notebooks and README.
- Official IOAI 2024 direct zip assets from IOAI resources.
- Public CEOAI/EUROAI 2026 Practice Round 1 and Practice Round 2 statements from Nitro Judge.
- Public GitHub repositories from Poland, Kazakhstan, Romania, NEOAI, Hungary, and Malaysia/China training sources.
- Public Nitro ROAI 2026 selection-camp statements for Polyglot, Too Easy Fairy, and Smart Warehouse.

Indexed only:

- Kaggle, Bohrium, Google Drive, or login-gated assets.
- Overview articles without downloadable task assets.
- IOAI-only audio tasks, unless useful as context.

## CEOAI Inference Rule

There are no past CEOAI problems yet. Any "expected CEOAI problem" note here is an inference from:

- local `olympiads/ceoai_syllabus.md`;
- official IOAI task format;
- national/regional AI olympiad selection tasks;
- the fact that CEOAI is a regional IOAI-aligned olympiad.

Treat the highest-yield expected style as: fixed data, notebook workflow, metric or leaderboard-style score, validation/submission format, and limited environment.

## Fast Use

1. Open `practice_queue.md`.
2. Pick the first unfinished task that maps to your current weak CEOAI section.
3. Open the linked local notebook or source URL.
4. Produce one visible artifact before moving on.
5. If you make a reusable mistake, log it in `olympiads/reviews/error_journal.jsonl`.

Do not spend CEOAI sprint time polishing the archive itself.
